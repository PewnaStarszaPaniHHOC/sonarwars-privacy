🔒 Sonar Wars — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sonar Wars ("the Game"), developed and published by Illusory Moon ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information directly. The Game does not require account registration, login, or any form of personal identification.

The following data may be stored locally on your device only:

  • Game settings and preferences
  • In-game progress and earned rewards
  • Unlocked in-game content

This data is never transmitted to us or any third party by us directly.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game may integrate third-party services that have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the data practices of these third parties. Please review their respective privacy policies for more information.

Google AdMob

The Game may display optional rewarded video advertisements through Google AdMob. If you choose to watch an ad, Google may collect certain information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

4. Online Gameplay

The Game offers optional online multiplayer functionality. When using online features, a temporary connection is established to facilitate gameplay. Only the minimum technical data necessary to maintain the connection and enable gameplay is processed. No personal information, usernames, or accounts are required or stored. Connection data is not retained after the session ends.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 or the applicable age in your jurisdiction. As we do not collect personal data, no special provisions are required.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the Game. All locally stored game data remains on your device and is under your control at all times.

7. Data Retention

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you maintain full control over it. Uninstalling the Game will remove all locally stored data from your device.

8. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Delete your data — by uninstalling the Game
  • Opt out of personalized ads — through your device's advertising settings
  • Contact us — with any questions or concerns regarding your data

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
